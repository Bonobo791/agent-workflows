# Reel pipeline

The reel in `marketing/`: a 30-second vertical video in which a presenter speaks a script in the brand voice. The voiceover is made by the pipeline; the owner makes the avatar clip from it by hand.

## Language

**Reel**:
The 30-second vertical video made from the approved script, the avatar clip and full-frame scenes.
_Avoid_: short, video

**Script**:
The words the presenter says in the reel, in one take.
_Avoid_: copy, voiceover text

**Audio tag**:
A bracketed delivery cue from a short list (such as [laughs]), heard in the voiceover and never shown.
_Avoid_: stage direction

**Voiceover**:
The recording of the script made by ElevenLabs in the speaking voice. The owner makes the avatar clip from it.
_Avoid_: audio, narration

**Avatar clip**:
The talking-head video of the presenter speaking the voiceover, made by hand by the owner. Its sound is the reel's sound.
_Avoid_: presenter video, render

**Presenter**:
The character on screen and in the voice.
_Avoid_: avatar (that is the clip), host, narrator

**Character**:
The brand file that defines the presenter: name, personality, speaking voice, and what they do and don't do.
_Avoid_: persona

**Speaking voice**:
The ElevenLabs voice the voiceover is spoken in. For Moderaty it is the owner's own professional voice clone.
_Avoid_: voice (on its own)

**Brand voice**:
How the brand writes and talks: tone and words. The moderaty-voice skill.
_Avoid_: voice (on its own)

**Hand-off**:
The pause in the reel step where the run waits for the owner's avatar clip.

**Subtitles**:
The word-timed text shown on the reel, taken from the voiceover.
_Avoid_: captions (the caption is the social post's text)
