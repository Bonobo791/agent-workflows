# Video titles

Your draft holds every section of the package. This pass decides the Titles section and the words of the first frame. Later passes keep them.

## The two gates (pass or fail)
1. **Truth**: will the reel deliver what the lead title promises by the payoff? If not, change the title, never the truth.
2. **Pull**: read cold, would a stranger need to watch? If not, it is not a title yet.

## Titles
- Three titles, one per line under "## Titles". The first is the lead, the title the Short carries.
- Each title is at most 60 characters.
- Promise, not label: a concrete noun plus a curiosity or a stake.
- Never a pure mystery ("This changes everything") and never a dry label.
- The lead title and the on-screen text of the first frame share no word. The title says what the reel is about; the first frame says the hook. The same promise, in different words.
- No keyword stuffing and no AI vocabulary.
- Use a number only when the brief supplies it.

## Output
Write only the sections the step asks for. Do not report scores, formula names, checklists, questions or hand-offs, and do not mention scripts or tools. The examples in the references are shapes: use only the brief's Facts, never an example's numbers, names or results.
