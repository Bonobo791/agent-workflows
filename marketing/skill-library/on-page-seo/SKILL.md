# On-page SEO craft

Make one page clear to a search engine and to a reader who arrived from a search.

1. Title: put the primary keyword near the start. Make it specific and inviting, and make it differ from other titles on the same site.
2. Meta description: include the keyword naturally. Say what the reader gets, and end with a clear reason to click.
3. Address and slug: keep it short and descriptive, use hyphens, and include the keyword. Leave out identifiers and parameters.
4. Headings: use one main heading that carries the keyword. Use subheadings that cover the subtopics and related terms. Keep the order logical, with no skipped levels. Write natural language, never a list of keywords.
5. Content: cover the topic completely for the search intent. Answer the question the reader came with in the first paragraph. Add something that the pages already ranking do not offer. Use related terms where they fit.
6. Images: write alt text that describes what the image shows, and include the keyword only where it is natural.
7. Internal links: link to related pages with descriptive anchor text. Never use "click here" or "read more".
8. Structured data: use the markup type that matches the content.
9. Lists, tables and short definitions are the formats most often chosen for featured results. Use one when the content is a list, a comparison or a definition.
10. Show experience, expertise, authority and trust in the text itself: say who did the work and how they know, cite sources, and name the limits of the advice.

Never stuff the keyword. A sentence that reads worse for the repetition is wrong, however many times the keyword appears. Write for the reader first and the search engine second.
