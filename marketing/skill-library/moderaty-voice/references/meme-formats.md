## 0. How to use this bank

1. Name the job: social post, headline, email, reply, microcopy.
2. Pick a format from section 3 that fits the job and platform (section 9).
3. Fill it with Moderaty nouns from section 1. Specific beats generic: name the archetype, give the fake quote, give the verdict.
4. Add one mechanic from section 2.
5. Cut to one joke per line. Keep the strongest, delete the rest.

Rules for all material below:
- A format that feels stale to the audience loses. If unsure, pick a different one.

## 2. Joke mechanics (formulas)

1. **Mock-formal.** Treat a troll like a court case or a corporate memo. "The court notes the defendant said 'L + ratio' four minutes after upload."
2. **Understatement.** Describe chaos as mild. "Technically polite. Spiritually exhausting."
3. **Rule of three with a swerve.** Two real items, third is absurd. "Spam, scams, and a man named Chad who has an opinion about your lighting."
4. **Bait and switch.** Warm setup, rude pivot. "Love from Brazil, this helped me so much… also check out my crypto channel."
5. **Over-literal.** Take the feature name literally. "Held for review. Gently. Like a hug, but for spam."
6. **Specificity.** The odd number or object is the joke. "Four hundred words correcting your thumbnail."
7. **Callback.** Reuse "bonk", "Chad", "touch grass" later in the same piece.
8. **Fake-humble flex.** Brag about restraint. "We have never once posted as you. We have standards."
9. **Escalation ladder.** Three reactions, each bigger. "Hold. Reject. Delete. Ban. Touch grass."
10. **Direct address to the troll.** "No you didn't, Chad."
11. **Meme grammar on a boring feature.** Describe an audit log in a meme format. "Receipts: every bonk, timestamped."
12. **Fake UI/notification.** Show a mock queue line. "@sub4sub_andy: REJECTED. rule: REGEX promo pattern."
13. **Contrast of scale.** Tiny tool, huge dramatic reaction. "$5 a month. Cheaper than the therapy."

## 3. Format library

Each entry: what it is, template, Moderaty examples, watch out.

### 3.1 Things To Say
Headshot or mascot plus a list of deliberately terrible advice for one situation, presented as sincere. Template: "THINGS TO SAY TO [target]" + 4 to 6 bullets, each worse than the last.
- THINGS TO SAY TO THE CRYPTO BOT: "Chad, I know it's you." / "How's the 8,412 dollars?" / "Is that the same link as last video?" / "The hammer is already warm."
- THINGS TO SAY TO A CREATOR AFTER 400 COMMENTS: "I'm fine." / "I didn't read them." / "I only read the first one." / "It was 'first!!'."
- THINGS TO SAY TO THE ACKCHYUALLY GUY: "You're right about 4:12." / "You're also right about everything else." / "Please take it to the review queue."

### 3.2 Top 5 horror movies
Ordinary dread ranked as horror films. Template: "Top 5 horror movies:" then 5 mundane horrors.
- "1. Opening notifications at 3 a.m. 2. 'Ackchyually…' 3. 'sub4sub anyone?' 4. The Brigadier arriving four minutes after upload. 5. Reading the comments."
- Landing page version: a section titled "Our top 5 horror films" listing the five troll types, followed by one plain sentence about the workflow.

### 3.3 The saxophones are getting louder
A calm situation sliding toward inevitable disaster. Template: "[normal moment], and the saxophones keep getting louder."
- "You upload a video about your day off, and the saxophones keep getting louder."
- "You open a drama video's comments, and the saxophones keep getting louder."
- "Moderaty at 2 a.m.: 47 incoming, 41 actioned, saxophones off."
Watch out: use as video caption or short post, ideally with a calm creator face.

### 3.4 Imagine hating on me
A harmless or wholesome activity with a dramatic caption. Template: "imagine hating on [harmless thing]."
- Creator calmly petting a capybara: "imagine hating on this. (the Brigadier did. he's in the queue.)"
- "Imagine hating on a man who is just uploading a sourdough video."
Watch out: keep the creator unbothered; joke is the troll's absurd target.

### 3.5 I thought we were having a nice date
A good start that gets derailed. Template: "[warm line]… [pivot to disaster]."
- "love from Brazil, this helped me so much" then "also check out my crypto channel."
- "Coming from your older video, great content!" then "anyway sub4sub."
- Landing page: show the nice comment, then the rejected promo, then "REJECTED: rule: REGEX promo pattern."

### 3.6 I need therapy. The therapy:
Setup "I need therapy", reveal a small comfort. Template: "I need therapy. The therapy: [product]."
- "I need therapy. The therapy: a review queue with six comments instead of 47."
- "I need therapy. The therapy: $5 a month."

### 3.7 Suspect lineup
Friends or family narrated like suspects with quirky traits. Template: police-lineup voiceover listing habits.
- "Suspect one: 'first!!' every video. Suspect two: 'ackchyually.' Suspect three: has never once watched the video."
- Carousel: each slide an archetype with a "charges" line and a verdict stamp (held, deleted, approved).

### 3.8 The Great Fandom Truce (rivals unite)
Rival groups join forces against one common target. Template: "[Group A] and [Group B] have put aside their differences to deal with [common enemy]."
- "Gaming channels and cooking channels have put aside their differences to deal with the crypto bot."
- "Every creator, every niche, one common enemy: the same self-promo pasted under every video."
Watch out: the common target must be bots or spam.

### 3.9 Meme dump / "the memes I showed my therapist this week"
A carousel of saved memes with a joking caption on the first slide. Template: cover line plus 5 to 10 slides.
- Cover: "memes I showed my moderation queue this week (part 11)." Slides: archetype quotes with verdict stamps.
Watch out: first slide must promise the swipe. Keep each slide one joke.

### 3.10 Half-skilled horse
Half beautifully drawn, half crude, labeled with two skills. Template: "[skill A] / [skill B]."
- Realistic half: "making videos." Crude half: "reading the comments."
- Realistic half: "writing rules." Crude half: "remembering to log in."

### 3.11 Your month, your…
A 12-slide carousel assigning one item per birth month. Template: "your month, your [theme]."
- "your month, your troll archetype": January the Brigadier, February the Crypto Bot, and so on.
Watch out: swipeable and personal, each slide one line.

### 3.12 Millennial vs Gen Z marketing
Two versions of the same message, one earnest and long, one clipped and slangy. Template: two columns.
- Earnest: "Moderaty checks published comments, top-level and replies, on your connected YouTube channels with rules, AI scoring, and a review queue." Slangy: "rules swing first. AI mops up. you decide. no auto-replies. bonk."
Watch out: both sides should feel recognizable, not mocked.

### 3.13 Mock-serious headline, absurd content
A news-style headline that turns absurd. Template: "Scientists reveal [absurd thing]."
- "Local creator reveals the hi."
- "Area man discovers comment section was bots the whole time."

### 3.14 Hold to see…
Interactive reel where viewers press and hold while images flash. Template: "Hold to see [outcome]."
- "Hold to see which troll you get today." Flash archetype cards. Final frame: the product CTA.

### 3.15 Buffering / blank-stare reaction
Face with a loading symbol, used for confusion. Template: "[question]. Me: [loading]."
- "Someone: 'what was your video about?' Me after reading 'first!!': [loading]."

### 3.16 Exhausted-face reaction
A drained, messy-haired face. Template: "[mundane burden]" in caption.
- "'i want to go home' is something you'll always catch me saying after reading comments."
- "Me after dealing with the Essay Commenter all day."

### 3.17 Oversized handcuffs (you could leave any time)
A character in comically huge handcuffs, free to slip out. Template: "how it feels to [voluntary torment]."
- "how it feels to read your own comments when Moderaty exists."
- "how it feels to keep arguing with the Ackchyually Guy."

### 3.18 Launched-into-the-air freeze frame
A figure frozen mid-launch, edited into scenes. Template: the figure plus a mundane label.
- The Edge Lord freeze-framed mid-launch labeled "bonked." Use original cartoon art.

### 3.19 The banner edit
Two figures holding a large black banner with your own message. Template: banner reads your announcement.
- Banner: "pricing page is live." Banner: "new rule: no more sub4sub." Use original art.

### 3.20 Sprinting pursuer
An intense sprinter in pursuit. Template: "Oh no, imagine [thing] catching up to you."
- "imagine the review queue catching up to you." Use original art.

### 3.21 A street full of X the second they hear Y
Hundreds of cats staring upward, mesmerized. Template: "A street full of [group] the second they hear [trigger]."
- "A street full of creators the second they hear 'new comment notification'."
- "A street full of trolls the second you disable replies."

### 3.22 Nostalgia slideshow ("Mom, what were you like in the 90s?")
A present-day clip followed by old photos set to a nostalgic song. Template: "[question]" then slideshow.
- "Creator, what were your comments like before Moderaty?" Then screenshots of scenes from a past era of mess (illustrative only, mocked-up).

### 3.23 Not-you imposter callout
Spotting an obvious fake. Template: "you're blatantly just not [real thing]."
- A bot account named after a creator's fan: "you're blatantly just not a real subscriber."

### 3.24 Gossip beats ("us gossiping about X and X walks by")
Two characters gossiping about a third who walks past. Template: "when [two people] gossip and [target] walks by."
- "When me and the review queue are gossiping about the Brigadier and he leaves a new comment."

### 3.25 Hero shot for a mundane struggle
A tiny character in armor with a sword for a trivial task. Template: "Me [trying to do mundane thing]."
- "Me entering the comment section." "Me surviving the last three months of 2026."

### 3.26 Sarcastic applause
Enthusiastic clapping for something terrible. Template: "[bad outcome]: clap."
- "The Edge Lord on his fourth 'peaked in 2016' comment." Applause.

### 3.27 Bad-idea face
A cat or character with a scheming expression. Template: "Me about to [bad idea]."
- "Me about to write a regex rule at midnight."
- "Me about to read the replies."

### 3.28 Brain-in-the-wrong-place anatomy
An x-ray with the brain in the wrong body part. Template: "How it feels talking to [target]."
- "How it feels reading 'first!!' on a video about quantum physics."

### 3.29 Setup, then "and… that's all"
A line where someone gets singled out or dismissed. Template: end a post with a flat dismissal.
- "Rules, AI scoring, human review… and the Brigadier. That's all."

### 3.30 Expectation vs reality / outfit-comparison jokes
Side-by-side comparisons. Template: "what they think vs what it is."
- "The Edge Lord thinks he's dark humor. The audit log thinks he's a Tuesday."

### 3.31 Custody-battle structure
"Whoever wins X gets custody of Y." Template: absurd stakes for a contest.
- "Whoever wins this comment war gets custody of the thumbnail."
- "If the regex beats the Brigadier, the regex gets custody of your Saturday."

### 3.32 Fandoms Grab Your Weapons chain
A call to assembled groups. Template: "[Group], grab your [thing]."
- "Moderators, grab your regex. Creators, grab your coffee. Capybara, stay calm."

### 3.33 Repeated absurd doorstep prank
A running gag with an unseen victim and a catchy chorus. Template: "[silly name], open up the door."
- "Cheesy Regex, open up the door." Then random rules on the handle.

### 3.34 Distracted conversation
A conversation derailed by one striking feature. Template: "trying to have a conversation with [distraction]."
- "trying to read my comments with the Brigadier in them."

### 3.35 Event meme reactions
Fast, timely jokes tied to a shared viewing moment. Template: react within the day, tie to the audience emotion, not the event itself.
- A big creator drama video drops: "me watching the drama video comments from the safety of my review queue."

### 3.36 Meta-commentary on marketing
Memes that mock marketing itself. Template: describe the ad or post as it really is.
- "Our marketing team after writing 'Never read another hate comment' and meaning it."

## 5. Slang bank

Use sparingly, one or two per piece, and never explain them.
- **bonk / bonked**: moderation action.
- **touch grass**: sign-off for trolls and burned-out creators.
- **L + ratio**: troll's own weapon turned back on him ("L + ratio + held for review").
- **W / L labels**: verdict stamps. "W: approved. L: the Brigadier."
- **cope**: said by the Edge Lord, answered by the regex.
- **mogged**: outclassed. "The Edge Lord got mogged by a keyword rule."

- **ackchyually**: pedantic correction opener.
- **No you didn't, Chad.**: universal answer to a crypto claim.
