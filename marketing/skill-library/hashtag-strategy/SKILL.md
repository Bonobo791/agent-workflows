# Hashtag craft

Hashtags label a post. They help a platform file it under a topic. They do not make a post reach people on their own. The words in the caption, the on-screen text and the spoken words do the discovery.

1. Start with the keywords. Make sure the caption carries the phrase a reader would search for. Hashtags come after that, not instead of it.
2. Choose a few hashtags that name the topic of this post and its audience. Prefer specific topic labels to broad ones.
3. Do not use generic tags that attract unrelated readers, such as the tags for "for you" feeds or for going viral. Do not add a long list of tags to cover every possibility.
4. Use a branded hashtag only for a campaign or a community the post belongs to, and keep it short, unique and easy to spell.
5. Rotate sets of hashtags across posts. Do not paste the same block under every post.
6. Match the hashtags to the post. A hashtag that the post does not match misleads readers and does more harm than good.

Before the hashtags are final, check: is the keyword in the caption; does each hashtag name this post's topic; are there any generic tags to remove; is the set specific to this post rather than copied from another.
