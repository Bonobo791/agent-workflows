# TikTok

The For You Page is an interest graph, not a follower graph: follower count and past hits are not direct ranking inputs, so every upload is re-tested on its own metadata, sound and early signals. All benchmarks below are third-party or creator-analysis estimates unless stated; TikTok has not published seed sizes or expansion thresholds.

## 1. Ranking signals (TikTok's three buckets)
1. **User interactions**: likes, shares, comments, follows, completion, rewatches, saves, content the viewer creates. Heaviest weights live here; completion, saves, shares and rewatches outweigh likes; comment threads with replies beat lone one-liners; click-through to profile signals the video bonded viewer to creator.
2. **Video information**: captions (first 100 to 150 characters do the heaviest lifting), sounds, hashtags, on-screen text, transcripts. It tells the algorithm who to seed against, so every hook and caption does double duty.
3. **Device and account settings**: language, country, device. Filters the candidate pool more than it ranks.
Test-then-expand: small seed cohort (existing followers tend to be seeded first, so weak first-day follower engagement gives the algorithm fewer reasons to expand; a format pivot can cost a few rough uploads) → read completion, rewatch, save, share, comment, follow-through → expand or park. Stale videos can re-enter testing after new shares, so do not delete underperformers (archive if embarrassing).
Does not reward: raw like counts, watch-time padding, hashtag stuffing, posting frequency for its own sake (repetitive low-effort publishing is enforced against). The US runs retrained recommendation infrastructure separate from the global model, so diversify across platforms.

## 2. Hooks that survive the 3-second cliff
TikTok viewers are mid-scroll, you inherit zero attention, and the second-3 drop is the cleanest signal. Sound is usually on, so the hook works muted (visual) and audible (spoken line). A channel-style intro signals a format the FYP does not prioritize.
- **Middle of action**: open inside the high-stakes moment (for example a specific day and a swap; use only the brief's Facts).
- **Pattern interrupt / contrarian**: curiosity-gap hooks hold the 3-second mark better than descriptive ones; payoff must defend the claim.
- **Confession**: "I work in coffee. I'm not supposed to like this. I use it every day."
- **Withhold / product-tease**: show the effect, hold the product; viewers stay and ask "what is it?".
- **Visual + verbal compound**: strong visual plus a strong line in about 2 seconds stops sound-on and muted scrollers at once.
- **Sensory anchor**: ASMR, texture, unboxing novelty stops the scroll before language.
- **In-media-res result**: start on the after, back-fill the story.
Generate 6 to 10 across archetypes, label them, lead with payoff or stake, make line one concrete, say it aloud (cut if over about 2 seconds), pair with a 3 to 5 word bold on-screen hook.

## 3. Retention math by length (working bars, directional)
| Length | Target completion | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| under 15s | >100% | must loop; viewers replay before swiping |
| 15 to 30s | about 70% | classic sweet spot |
| 30 to 60s | about 50% | needs at least one mid-video re-hook |
| 60s+ | about 40% | two retention resets and a payoff stack |
Creator analyses (third-party) report holding 85%+ past second 3 pulls several times the views of under 60%; 21 to 34s clips show the highest raw completion (about 62% vs about 48% for 60s+). Rewatch rate of 20 to 30% on short clips reads as a strong quality signal; a looped ending raises completion versus a non-looping equivalent. Short for reach, 60s+ for paid views: build the format short for reach, then make a longer cut of the strongest hits for monetization, and do not publish both lengths of the same clip within days.

## 4. Script shapes by length
**21 to 34s (default cold reach):** 0 to 3s HOOK (payoff/stake; visual + line) · 3 to 8s ESCALATION 1 (the number or named thing) · 8 to 18s ESCALATION 2 (raise stakes; one re-hook around second 12 if energy plateaus) · 18 to 28s PAYOFF (visible result) · 28 to 34s LOOP or one CTA.
**Over 60s (Creator Rewards needs videos over 60 seconds):** HOOK 0 to 3 · PROMISE STACK 3 to 10 ("here is what you'll see") · ESCALATION 1 10 to 25 (open loop) · RE-HOOK 25 to 28 · ESCALATION 2 28 to 45 (close loop 1) · PAYOFF 45 to 52 · LOOP 52 to 62.
**90s+:** HOOK promises the payoff · SETUP 3 to 15 (stakes, one loop) · RE-HOOK 1 15 to 18 · ACT 1 18 to 45 · RE-HOOK 2 45 to 50 · ACT 2 50 to 75 · PAYOFF 1 75 to 85 · PAYOFF 2 85 to 95 · LOOP/CTA. Hardest points: seconds 25 to 35 and 60 to 70, so plan a visual reset at each.
Do not stretch; pad-stretched videos lose on completion.
Save-bait patterns: a list of 3 or 5, "send this to a friend who…", "save this for next time you…", a concrete payoff visible at the end.

## 5. Sound strategy
- Audio is searchable IP, trending audio carries a seed signal, and TikTok viewers usually play with sound.
- Trending audio for cold reach; original audio for brand recognition, voiceover-led formats, diegetic demo sound (ASMR, unboxing), signature lines, and anything you want others to reuse.
- The 48 to 72 hour window: adopt a trending sound early (Creative Center sounds page filtered to "Breakout" and your region; the same sound twice in one scroll session with under about 10k uses and climbing; niche creator chats). Log sounds with a ride-or-skip deadline.
- Match sound energy to the opening visual; land the payoff on or just after the beat drop; avoid a silent first half-second (reads as a broken upload).
- Account type is binding: Creator accounts get the full licensed library; Business accounts only the Commercial Music Library (laggier) and lose Creator Rewards eligibility. Switching Business → Creator does not reset followers or analytics.
- Trending TikTok audio rarely transfers to Reels or Shorts (copyright friction); re-export with platform-native or original audio.

## 6. Native features
- **Duets**: help when reacting to a rising video in your niche within about 24 hours and adding new context; fail as silent reactions or off-niche borrowed reach.
- **Stitches**: clip 1 to 5s then answer ("you asked, I answered", "here's what they got wrong", "and then this happened"). The source clip is your hook and takes the 3-second cliff, so pick a tight 2 to 3 second question, claim or cliffhanger.
- **Shoppable video**: problem-first, product in frame by second 6, real-use demo, soft close (the card does the work); native-feeling beats polished ad creative.
- **LIVE highlights**: treat a LIVE as raw material; mark clean product-in-frame demo moments so auto-clips are good; check Seller Center afterward.
- **Symphony AI tools**: AI dubbing and image-to-video B-roll can help; skip realistic AI people/places/events, AI-generated hooks (a synthetic voice/face in the first 3 seconds tends to collapse the hold), and avatar-only formats.

## 7. Anti-patterns that quietly demote
TikTok watermark on Reels/Shorts cross-posts (reported reach reductions up to about 40%); repetitive low-effort AI content; hashtag stuffing and #fyp as strategy; delayed-hook intros (logo, "hey guys welcome back", "today I'm going to talk about…", a musical sting before the first line); Business account for a creator who wants trending sounds; deleting low performers; pad-stretching to 60s; cross-posting without re-shooting the hook.
Use instead: "save this for next time you…", "send to a friend who…", "which part would you change?", or no CTA and let the loop carry it.

## 8. Monetization and Shop
- **Creator Rewards** (source-reported eligibility; verify current rules): 10,000 followers, 100,000 qualifying views in 30 days, age 18+, account in an eligible country (US, UK, Germany, France, Japan, Brazil), Creator account, videos over 60 seconds. Reported RPM about $0.20 to $1.00 standard, higher in finance/tech/education.
- **TikTok Shop**: seller or affiliate. Source-reported commissions: electronics about 5%, home about 8 to 12%, US average about 13%, beauty about 15 to 30%. Source-reported affiliate share of US Shop GMV about 42%.
- Problem-first content: hook on the problem, product on screen by second 6 (source-reported brand-affinity lift of about 65% for on-screen product), demo solves it, soft close.
- Funnel: visibility content (wide reach, no push) → trust content (founder, behind the scenes, real use, customer voices) → conversion content (shoppable). Direct-to-conversion without the trust layer converts far worse.
- Shape: 21 to 34s with a card for cold reach; 45 to 60s trust demo with the card visible throughout.
- Client work: never promise FYP placement; quote in saves and shop clicks as well as views; set the 48 to 72 hour expectation. Template: `assets/shop-video-template.md`.

## 9. Hard rules
No logo/greeting in frame 1; payoff or stake first; 3 to 5 specific hashtags; one CTA (save/send/comment) or a loop; Creator account for trending sounds; strip foreign watermarks; do not stretch to 60s.
