# Video captions

This pass sets the Caption, the Hashtags and the Pinned comment. Keep the other sections as they are.

## Caption (TikTok)
- The first 100 characters do the most work: they show before the cut and they help the video rank in search. The first line states the topic in plain words.
- The caption adds context the reel does not say. It does not repeat the on-screen text.
- One ask, matched to the content: a send, a save, or a watch to the end. Name the person or the moment it is for. Put it after a line of value, not in the opening seconds.
- Never write "Link in bio".

## Hashtags
Three to five, on one line, specific to the reel's topic.

## Pinned comment
One line with [ARTICLE LINK] and one specific reason to click. The reason is the content. It asks for nothing.

## Output
Write only the sections the step asks for. Do not report scores, formula names, checklists, questions or hand-offs, and do not mention scripts or tools. The examples in the references are shapes: use only the brief's Facts, never an example's numbers, names or results.
