# Instagram search craft: keywords, not hashtags

Instagram search matches the words in a post. The caption, the on-screen text, the words spoken early in a video and the image's description all count. Write so that a reader who searches for the topic finds the post.

1. Find the real queries. Think of what a reader would type. Start from the brief's Keywords. Build a small cluster of related phrases. Never invent search volumes or rankings.
2. Put the primary keyword in the first sentence of the caption, in natural language. Weave the related phrases through the rest of the caption where they fit.
3. Write the caption as searchable sentences. Say what the post is about in plain words.
4. Make the picture or video match the caption. A search or a viewer who sees a mismatch leaves. Words on screen should use the same keyword.
5. Do not stuff keywords. A caption that repeats a phrase until it reads badly is worse for the reader and is down-ranked.
6. Do not add hashtags that have nothing to do with the post. Hashtags only label the post.
7. Remove any watermark from another app.

Before the caption is final, check: is the primary keyword in the first sentence; do the words on screen match it; does the caption read naturally; does the post match the caption.
