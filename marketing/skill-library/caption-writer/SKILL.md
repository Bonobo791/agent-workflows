# Caption craft

A caption is part of the post that does work: it earns the tap, carries the message and asks for one action. It complements the picture or video rather than describing it.

1. The first line is the whole game. Readers see only the first line before they decide to read on. Write it to win the second line. Never open with a greeting, a hashtag, a date pleasantry, or a restatement of what the picture shows.
2. Pick one goal and let it set the structure: awareness, comments, saves, clicks or community. Let the goal decide the call to action.
3. Choose one format: a story, a how-to, a strong opinion, a question, a behind-the-scenes note, a result, or an announcement. Let the format give the caption its shape.
4. Write the body in the brand's voice. Deliver what the first line promised. Use line breaks where the reader needs to scan.
5. Use exactly one call to action that matches the goal. Two calls to action split the reader's action and weaken both.
6. Where a link cannot be clicked, direct the reader to the link in the profile.
7. Let the caption and the visual support each other. If the visual shows a thing, the caption explains why it matters.
8. Write three or four candidate first lines, choose the one that most makes a stranger need the next line, and build the body from it.

Before the caption is final, check: does the first line stop the scroll on its own; does it sound like the brand; is there one call to action that matches the goal; does the caption add to the visual; does it contain no greeting and no restated description?
