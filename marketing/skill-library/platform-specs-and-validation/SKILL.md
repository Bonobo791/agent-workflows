# Platform fit: check the draft against where it will appear

A draft is right only for the platform it is written for. Before the draft is final, check it against that platform's rules for text, media and layout.

1. Know the platform's rules for the format you are writing: how long the text may be, which media it accepts and in what shape, and which parts of the picture or video the platform crops or covers.
2. Keep the important content inside the area the platform shows. A face, a headline or a call to action that sits at an edge may be cropped away.
3. Put a link where a reader can click it on that platform. Where links cannot be clicked in the text, direct the reader to the link another way.
4. Make sure every element the platform requires is present: the text, the media, and any required description.
5. Check the draft against the step's contract. The contract states the exact limits for this workflow. When your knowledge of a platform and the contract differ, the contract wins.
6. Do not make a claim or a promise that the platform's rules prohibit, such as a reward for engagement or a claim of guaranteed results.

Before the draft is final, check: is it written for this platform; is the important content inside the visible area; is any link clickable; is every required element present; does it agree with the contract.
