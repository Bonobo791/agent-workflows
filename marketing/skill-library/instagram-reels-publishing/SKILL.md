# Reel layout: how Instagram shows a vertical video

The same vertical video is shown in three places: full screen in the Reels feed, cropped in the main feed, and cropped again in the profile grid. Lay out the video so that the important parts survive every crop.

1. Keep the critical content in the centre of the frame. The top and bottom edges are covered by the platform's interface and by the crops.
2. Keep the hook words, the faces and the call to action clear of the top and bottom edges.
3. Make any cover image carry its key text in the central area, because the grid crops the edges.4. Put any burned-in captions in the central area too, so they are not covered.
5. Remove any watermark from another app. The platform may reduce the reach of a video that carries one.
6. The reel has no audio. Every key point must be on screen as text.
7. The caption must match the video. Keep the same keyword in the caption and in the first on-screen text.

Before the layout is final, check: is the critical content in the centre; do the hook, the faces and the call to action survive the crops; does the cover carry its text in the central area; are any captions in the central area; is there any other app's watermark.
