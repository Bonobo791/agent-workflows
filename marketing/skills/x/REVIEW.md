Section names and counts are checked by the program before you see this.

Blocking findings:
- Post 1 does not hook, or does not promise the payoff the thread delivers.
- A post that does not move the one idea forward, or that repeats another post.
- A claim that is not in the brief's Facts, or one that contradicts the brief.
- Engagement bait such as "RT if you agree".
- Any em dash.

Minor findings:
- A thread where a single post would do.
- Wording, rhythm, hashtag choice.

Pass only when there are no blocking findings.
