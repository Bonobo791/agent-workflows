The program has already checked the items listed under Checked. Do not report those.

Checked:
- The posts are numbered 1/ to N/ in order.
- The thread has at most three posts; each post is 280 characters or fewer, counting [ARTICLE LINK] as 23.
- [ARTICLE LINK] appears exactly once, in the last post.
- The thread has at most two hashtags.
- The first line of each post uses none of: may, might, describes, positions, offers, various.
- The post uses none of: fictional, says it, stated, describes, positions, within that scope, supplied.
- No banned phrase (the brief, supplied facts, supplied evidence, provided facts, based on the facts, in this comparison, unassessed, the cited, cited documentation, this article, fictional, editorial, left unassessed); no "X, not Y", "not X, it's Y" or "rather than" contrast; and no sentence starts with the same two words as more than three others.

Judge as the target reader scrolling a busy feed. Passing the rules is not enough; a post that is accurate but forgettable is a revise.

Blocking findings:
- Line 1 would not stop a creator scrolling: it describes a situation with no stakes, no number and no tension.
- The post covers more than one idea, or reads as a summary of the article.
- No opinion or recommendation.
- A stronger hook was passed over in ## Hooks.
- Hooks in ## Hooks that share one reader problem.
- More than 2 facts in the post.
- A post whose main point is a product limit or a reason not to buy.
- Post 1 does not hook, or does not promise the payoff the thread delivers.
- A post that does not move the one idea forward, or that repeats another post.
- A claim that is not in the brief's Facts, or one that contradicts the brief's Facts or its Channel idea for X.
- Engagement bait such as "RT if you agree".
- Any em dash.
- The first line of post 1 names a product or feature, not the reader's problem or a concrete moment.
- The thread is mainly a feature list with no example or scene.
- A banned hedge word in the hook (may, might, describes, positions, offers, various).
- Copy that mentions the brief, supplied facts or supplied evidence.
- A hook or opening built on a problem that is not in positioning.md's Reader problems.
- A "not X, it's Y" or "X, not Y" contrast.
- Meta talk about sources or the comparison, such as "based on the facts" or "in this comparison".
- An instruction to the writer in the copy, such as "Give the native workflow a clear recommendation".
- The same advice repeated in more than two sections.

Minor findings:
- A thread where a single post would do.
- Wording, rhythm, hashtag choice.

Pass only when there are no blocking findings.
