The program has already checked the items listed under Checked. Do not report those.

Checked:
- Line 1 starts with "Meta description: ", and the meta description is 150 to 160 characters.
- There is exactly one "# " title line.
- The required headings appear as "## " headings in the required order.
- The word count is within 20% of the brief's target.
- The opening paragraph after the title is 3 sentences or fewer and gives the answer with "if" or "when".
- No six-word phrase appears three or more times.
- No more than three sentences start with the same two words.
- No banned phrase (the brief, supplied facts, supplied evidence, provided facts, based on the facts, in this comparison, unassessed, the cited, cited documentation, this article, fictional, editorial, left unassessed) in the article.
- No "X, not Y", "not X, it's Y" or "rather than" contrast.
- Every link to a page (a path starting with "/") is listed in pages.md.

Blocking findings:
- A claim that is not in the brief's Facts.
- An H2 from the brief's Article plan that is missing or out of order.
- The meta description lacks the primary keyword.
- The title lacks the primary keyword near the start.
- The opening paragraph does not answer the main question.
- A comparison article with no comparison table.
- A call to action that does not match the brief's Goal.
- Any em dash.
- The opening paragraph names a product or feature, not the reader's problem or a concrete moment.
- The article is mainly a feature list with no example or scene.
- A banned hedge word in the hook (may, might, describes, positions, offers, various).
- Copy that mentions the brief, supplied facts or supplied evidence.
- A hook or opening built on a problem that is not in positioning.md's Reader problems.
- A link to a page not listed in pages.md.
- A "not X, it's Y" or "X, not Y" contrast.
- Meta talk about sources or the comparison, such as "based on the facts" or "in this comparison".
- An instruction to the writer in the copy, such as "Give the native workflow a clear recommendation".
- The same advice repeated in more than two sections.

Minor findings:
- Passive voice, filler, adverbs, flow.
