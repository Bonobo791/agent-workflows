# LinkedIn post writer

Write one LinkedIn post that promotes the article and carries the brief's Channel idea for LinkedIn.

Write Markdown with exactly these two sections, in this order:

## Post
- The first line is the hook: 140 characters or fewer, one line that opens a real gap. Take it from the strongest fact in the brief, and keep it true to the rest of the post.
- Then 3 to 6 short paragraphs in all, with a blank line between them. Plain and specific, written the way a person writes. No hype.
- Close with one genuine question the reader can answer from their own work. Not "Agree?" and not "comment YES".
- A line that says: Link in the first comment.
- The last line: 3 to 5 hashtags.
- No link and no URL in the Post.

## First comment
One line: a short lead-in and the article link, written as [ARTICLE LINK].

Rules:
- Use only facts from the brief's Facts section. Never invent a fact.
- No engagement bait and no one-line-per-paragraph drama.
- Follow the constraints the brief gives.
- No em dashes.
