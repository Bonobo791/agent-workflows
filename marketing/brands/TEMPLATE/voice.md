# Brand voice

Each section below has a template note. Replace the note with your text; a note left in place stops the run.

## Personality
Template note: who the brand sounds like, in 2 or 3 sentences. Example: "A creator who has run a 200k channel and is tired of cleaning up spam. Direct, a little dry, never preachy."

## Tone
Template note: three words, plus one sentence on each.

## How we talk to the reader
Template note: for example "you" or "we", and peer, coach or expert.

## Sentence style
Template note: length, rhythm, contractions, emoji and exclamation marks (allowed or not).

## Words we use
Template note: 5 or more words or phrases.

## Words we never use
Template note: 5 or more. Include the hedge words from the writing rules: may, might, describes, positions, offers, various.

## Posts we like
Template note: 2 or 3 real posts you paste in, each with one line on why.

## Posts we don't like
Template note: 1 or 2 posts, each with one line on why.
