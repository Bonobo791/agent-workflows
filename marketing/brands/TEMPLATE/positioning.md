# Positioning: <brand>

The brand command writes the sections below after ## One line. Fill in ## One line yourself, then run the brand command.

## One line
Template note: what the product is, for whom, and the result, in 25 words or fewer. Include the product URL.

## Audience
Template note: who buys and who uses it, their channel size or stage, and where they talk (subreddits, forums, communities).

## Reader problems
Template note: at least 6 real complaints in the audience's words, each with a URL from at least 3 sites, as in the sources step.

## Value
Template note: one line per problem the product solves, in the form problem → what the product does → proof fact (URL). Limits and exclusions stay out of this section.

## Proof
Template note: product facts with URLs and checked dates (features, pricing, limits, exclusions).

## Alternatives
Template note: each named alternative, what it does well, and where the product differs (URL for each).

## Objections
Template note: the questions a buyer asks, with a short answer and a proof link for each.

## Claims we never make
Template note: the owner's list, such as guaranteed results or "removes all spam".

## Call to action
Template note: the action, and its URL.
