# Brand voice

## Personality
Moderaty sounds like its founder, a YouTube creator who moderates their own comments and writes about it in first person ("I hold links", "my rule is"). The voice is funny and meme-literate, with a cast of comment archetypes: the Ackchyually Guy, the Edge Lord, the Crypto Bot, the Brigadier.

## Tone
- **Conversational (required):** every line MUST sound like one person talking to another. Never dry, stiff, formal or corporate. Social posts and video copy most of all.
- **Funny:** Jokes go in headlines, leads, eyebrows and fictional examples, aimed at trolls, spam bots and scammers, never at real people or genuine critics.

## How we talk to the reader
The author speaks as "I" and addresses the reader as "you", as one creator to another: peer, not expert or coach. The articles share what the author does ("My advice:", "I'd begin with a hold action") and leave the call to the reader ("You decide what stays on your own channel", "Your comment section, your house rules").

## Sentence style
- Short to medium sentences, mostly plain statements, with contractions (I'd, don't, you're).
- Jokes land as a short aside or closing clause: "(hi, mom)", "(probably crypto)", "curiosity is how you get pwned", "“4:12” is not a crime."
- No emoji and no exclamation marks in the two blog articles.
- SEO titles and meta descriptions stay plain; the joke goes in the visible headline.

- Go touch grass

- hold for review

## Words we never use
- may
- might
- describes
- positions
- offers
- various
- "Every other comment tool" (the homepage test rejects it)
- "so you don't have to" in the hero (the homepage test rejects it)
- "only look at" in the final call to action (the homepage test rejects it)
- "Never read another hate comment." anywhere but the homepage hero tagline
- slurs, censored or not

## Posts we like
1. From the hate-comments article: "Some comments come as a combo meal. A viewer spots a wrong price and adds an insult on the side. That is the Ackchyually Guy with extra salt, and he is sometimes right. I verify the price, fix it if needed, then apply my rule to the insult."
   Why: the joke uses the house cast, and the paragraph ends in a concrete moderation decision.

## Posts we don't like
Dry posts that just talk about how great the app is, has no personality, has no idea of what the audience wants to hear, or is simply too corporate-dry.
