7. Close with a practical next step: run one article through the process and keep the decision record.

Version and decision record

Link each item to the versions it depends on. Record who made each decision, what was approved or blocked, and why. Reopen affected checks when an upstream item changes; retain the prior decision history.

Checklist for the article

- Every factual claim, quotation and citation has verified support in an approved, version-recorded source; unsupported material is removed or blocked.

- A human has reviewed the draft and recorded the decision and unresolved findings.
- Version links connect brief, sources, draft, review, channel assets, package and decision.
- Hypothetical material is labeled; prompts do not imply that AI verification is sufficient.
